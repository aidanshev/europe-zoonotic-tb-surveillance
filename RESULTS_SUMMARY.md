# Results and claim boundary

The final vintage-correct panel contained 99 transitions and only 7 emergence events. The dynamic animal block did not establish incremental predictive value beyond human reporting history; its AUPRC gain was about 0.009 with clustered uncertainty spanning no benefit and a nonsignificant permutation analysis. The intended country-level EU-origin endpoint could not be reconstructed from exact public tables and failed closed. The supported interpretation is surveillance discordance/reporting prediction, not predicted spillover or source attribution.
