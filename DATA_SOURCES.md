# Data sources and acquisition

Raw third-party datasets are not mirrored here unless redistribution and privacy are clearly appropriate. Download from the official source, record retrieval date/version/size/SHA-256, and do not assume a newer download matches the historical analysis input.

| Source | Official site | Public-repository treatment |
|---|---|---|
| EFSA/ECDC EU One Health 2024 Zoonoses Report | https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/plain-language-summary/european-union-one-health-2024-zoonoses-report | Primary One Health report source. |
| EFSA biological monitoring of zoonoses | https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/data-report/biological-monitoring-zoonoses | Annual surveillance/report-series source. |
| WHO Tuberculosis Data | https://www.who.int/teams/global-tuberculosis-programme/data/ | TB denominator/context where used. |
